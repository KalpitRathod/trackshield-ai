How to Use TrackShield AI

Everything you need to know about the AI-era layoff recovery copilot

1

Overview: See the big picture

Start here. The Overview tab shows how many emails were processed, scams blocked, follow up reminders due, and active applications at a glance. Click any metric card to jump directly to that section.

2

Tracker: Your Kanban board

Every genuine email is classified into Applied, Interview, Offer, or Rejected. If you receive two emails from the same company (for example an acknowledgement and then an interview invite), they are automatically merged into one card and the status is upgraded.

3

Scam Shield: Fake offers caught in red

Any email asking for a training fee, Aadhaar or PAN before an interview, or arriving from a freemail domain (like Gmail pretending to be Infosys) is quarantined here. Scam status overrides every other classification: a fake "offer" is never added to your tracker.

4

Follow Ups: Never miss a chance

If an application has been in Applied status for 7 or more days with no reply, a follow up reminder is automatically raised. Click Draft Email to get a ready to send polite message. Use the date presets to simulate what reminders look like in the future.

5

Interview Prep: Strategic notes

On any card in the Interview column, click Prep Notes to get a tailored preparation brief: company background, core technical topics, likely interview questions with strategic answer tips, and smart questions to ask the interviewer.

6

Simulate different dates

Use the date picker in the top right corner to change what "today" means. This shows you exactly which follow ups would trigger on any given date. Try switching to Oct 31 to see 6 follow up reminders become due.

7

Offline first architecture

All Gemini API responses are pre-cached in gemini_cache.json. If Wi-Fi fails during the demo, the entire pipeline runs from disk and produces identical output. Live API calls auto-save back to the cache when connectivity is restored.

Scam Red Flag Reference

💸
Upfront Fee

Any demand for a training fee, registration charge, laptop deposit, or gate pass before joining. Real employers never charge candidates.

🪪
KYC Harvesting

Requests for Aadhaar card, PAN card, or cancelled cheque sent via email before any formal contract or background verification.

📧
Free-Mail Impersonation

Enterprise companies (TCS, Infosys, Wipro) will never recruit via @gmail.com or @yahoo.com. Always check the sender domain.

🔗
Lookalike Domain

Typosquatting domains like techmahindra-careers.co or tcs-careers-portal.com mimic real corporate sites.

💬
Telegram or WhatsApp

Legitimate interviews are on Google Meet, Zoom, or Teams. Moving to Telegram is a scam signal without exception.

⏰
Artificial Urgency

Phrases like "pay before 5 PM or forfeit your offer" are designed to prevent you from verifying the employer's identity.
